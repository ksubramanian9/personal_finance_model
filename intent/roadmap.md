# Roadmap

**Summary:** v1 proves the loop of choice, luck, outcome, understanding. Later versions add depth.

## v1: core loop
- 2 personas: Metro IT employee, Government employee
- Yearly engine, seeded RNG, macro regimes, about 8-10 life events, behavioral nudges
- 4-5 life stages with decision points; "Invest in yourself" as None / Light / Serious
- Instruments: EPF/PPF, equity SIP, FD, gold, home (buy/rent), basic insurance; simplified tax and capital gains
- Family choices (marriage, wedding level, children, parents)
- Decade cards expanding to years; headline outcome; retirement-age override
- Three disclosure levels; seed behind the advanced toggle

## v2: depth
- Debrief with same-seed counterfactuals
- Gig and Family-business personas
- Three capability tracks with effort/burnout sliders
- Luck dial

## v3: breadth
- Dual-income couple persona, NPS and more instruments
- Monte Carlo spread view
- Save and share runs

## Why this order
The debrief is the most valuable learning feature but needs a solid engine first. Two contrasting personas (high variance vs stability) exercise the engine best.
